# Evidence Standards (module template)

For any work where being wrong matters: research, reporting, analysis, records.

## Source grading

- **Primary:** the record itself, the original data, the person directly involved.
- **Secondary:** a compilation, index, or summary of primary material. Useful for finding primary sources. Never the final citation.
- **Tradition:** what is said, remembered, or repeated. Recorded as tradition, labeled as such, never upgraded to fact by repetition.

## Rules

1. A claim is only as strong as its best source. State the grade when you state the claim.
2. Silence in the record is a boundary. Say the record runs out. Never bridge a gap with a guess presented as fact.
3. Negative results are results. Log what you searched, where, and what was not found, so the search is never repeated blind.
4. When two sources conflict, keep both, name the conflict, and say what would resolve it.
5. Copies and exports are not sources. Cite what the copy was made from.

## Worked example (genealogy flavor; rewrite for your field)

A family tree website lists a man's birth year as 1902. The draft card he signed in 1942 also says 1902. The tree is secondary; the signed card is primary. The fact is recorded with the card as the citation, and the tree is noted as agreeing, not as the source.
