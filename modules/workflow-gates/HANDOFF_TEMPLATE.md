# Handoff Template

Use this when delegating a defined piece of work to another agent or a person. A good handoff is self-contained: the receiver needs nothing from the conversation it came from.

## Given / When / Then

- **Given:** [The starting state, verified. What exists, where, and how you know.]
- **When:** [The work to perform, in order.]
- **Then:** [Acceptance criteria, stated as checks anyone can run. Exhaustive, not sampled: "every file," not "the files."]

## Boundaries

- In scope: [files, systems, topics]
- Out of scope: [what must not be touched, even if it looks related]

## Stop rules

- Stop and report on a blocker. Do not improvise around one.
- Two attempts at the same failing step, then stop and report what was tried.
- Timebox: [state one]. Checkpoint your progress at the midpoint.

## Report back

- What changed, where, and the evidence for each claim.
- Anything left undone, and why.
